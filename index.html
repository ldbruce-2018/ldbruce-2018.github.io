Bio:
Dr. Bruce is a Principal Scientist, Analytics with Genesis Research Group. Research interests include HEOR/RWD cancer and chronic disease outcome measurement, veterans’ and military health, scale development in PROs including pain and social functioning, biostatistics, health economics, patient safety, and value-based decision making. Passionate about data, she uses SAS, R, SQL, Python, Teradata, and PowerBI, along with reproducible research and automation techniques to wrangle, build, curate, visualize, report, and model patient data for actionable insights.

Dr. Bruce earned her Ph.D. in Epidemiology from the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill, and completed a postdoctoral fellowship in Health Services Research at the Cecil G. Sheps Center, University of North Carolina. She has a Masters of Health Policy and Administration and Bachelor of Arts in Psychology from the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill.
